About Me

I'm a Director of Data Engineering in Risk at Charles Schwab, where I lead infrastructure teams building the data and ML platform that powers risk modeling across the enterprise. My work sits at the intersection of engineering leadership, data architecture, and applied ML.

I've been writing code professionally for nearly two decades — long enough to have built things in ExtJS before React existed, and short enough to still be the one opening pull requests. These days my stack is Python, C#, FastAPI, Next.js, and Blazor WASM, but honestly I'll pick up whatever gets the job done.

Outside of Schwab I build side projects, obsess over developer tooling, and hold FINRA Series 7, 63, and 24 licenses — a reminder that I've spent my whole career in financial services, where the stakes are real.
